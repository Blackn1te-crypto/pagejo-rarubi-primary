Client's Charter

The school undertakes to deliver on these commitments to our learners, parents, and community.

Pagejo Rarubi Primary School
The School Undertakes To
  1. TEACH, SUPERVISE, AND MARK ALL THE WORKING CURRICULUM.
  2. DEVELOP THE SOCIAL AND MORAL VALUES ACCEPTED IN SOCIETY DAILY.
  3. PARRICIPATE IN THE VARIOUS SPORTING DISCIPLINES STERNLY FOR FUTURE CARRIER PROSPECTS.
  4. SOURCE AND PROCURE AFFORDABLE AND APPROPRIATE TEACHING AND LEARNING MATERIAL.
  5. IDENTIFY ACCESS AND ASSIST PUPILS WITH SPECIAL NEEDS ON A DAILY BASIS.
  6. INTERACT WITH PARENTS ON CONSULTATION DAYS SPEECH PRIZE GIVING DAYS AND GENERAL MEETING.
  7. ASSIST CLIENTS COURTEOUSLY, EFFECTIVELY WITHIN THE SHORTEST POSSIBLE TIME.
  8. ANSWER PHONE CALLS WITHIN THE FIRST THREE (3) RINGS AND SPEND THE SHORTEST TIME POSSIBLE ON THE CALL.

Our Standards

Members of Staff Code of Conduct

Pagejo Rarubi Primary School
  • MEMBERS OF STAFF SHOULD BE HARD WORKING, TRUSTWORTHY AND HONEST.
  • MEMBERS SHOULD ALWAYS ACT IN LOCO PARENTIS.
  • MEMBERS SHOULD LOOK AFTER SCHOOL PROPERTY.
  • MEMBERS SHOULD MAINTAIN GOOD SCHOOL TONE.
  • MEMBERS SHOULD OBSERVE PUNCTUALITY.
  • MEMBERS SHOULD TAKE SAFETY PRECAUTIONS WHENEVER ENGAGING IN SCHOOL ACTIVITIES WITH PUPILS.
  • MEMBERS SHOULD OBSERVE THE OFFICIAL SECRET ACT.
  • MEMBERS SHOULD ADHERE TO ALL MINISTRY OF PRIMARY AND SECONDARY EDUCATION POLICIES.
