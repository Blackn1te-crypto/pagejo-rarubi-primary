Our Standards

Code of Conduct

CODE OF CONDUCT
PAGEJO RARUBI PRIMARY SCHOOL
  • LEARNERS MUST BE PUNCTUAL AND REGULAR IN ATTENDANCE.
  • RESPECT TOWARDS TEACHERS, STAFF, AND FELLOW LEARNERS IS MANDATORY.
  • SCHOOL PROPERTY MUST BE HANDLED WITH CARE AND RESPONSIBILITY.
  • BULLYING, FIGHTING, AND ANY FORM OF VIOLENCE IS STRICTLY PROHIBITED.
  • SCHOOL UNIFORM MUST BE WORN CORRECTLY AND WITH PRIDE.
  • HOMEWORK AND ASSIGNMENTS MUST BE COMPLETED AND SUBMITTED ON TIME.
  • LEARNERS MUST PARTICIPATE IN ALL SCHOOL ACTIVITIES AND PROGRAMS.
  • PARENTS ARE ENCOURAGED TO SUPPORT SCHOOL ACTIVITIES AND POLICIES.
