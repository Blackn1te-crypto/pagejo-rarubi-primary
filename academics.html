Our Procedures

Standards Operating Procedure

Pagejo Rarubi Primary School
  1. TEACHERS TO SUBMIT THEIR SCHEMES OF WORK ON OPENING DAY OF EVERY TERM.
  2. SCHEMES OF WORK ARE TO BE PREPARED USING HBC SYLLABI.
  3. LESSONS ARE TO BE CONDUCTED AS PER THE HBC TIME TABLE.
  4. ENSURE TO ABIDE BY SI 21 OF 2024 ON TEACHING WORK LOAD.
  5. EVALUATED SCHEMES OF WORK TO BE SUBMITTED TO THE D/HEAD'S OFFICE ON MONDAYS MORNING.
  6. PROFESSIONAL RECORDS ARE SUBMITTED TO THE DEPUTY HEAD'S OFFICE FORTNIGHTLY.
  7. REGISTERS TO BE SUBMITTED TO THE OFFICE AT 0900 ON MONDAYS.
  8. FORTNIGHT TESTS ARE WRITTEN AND RECORDED IN BOTH TESTS AND PROGRESS RECORDS.
