Welcome to Our School Family

At Pagejo Rarubi Primary School, we believe every child is a unique gift with boundless potential. Our dedicated team of educators works tirelessly to nurture not just academic excellence, but also strong moral values, life skills, and God-given talents.

We are guided by our Vision 2030 — towards a prosperous upper middle-income society — and our mission to provide a heritage-driven education where character building is paramount.
