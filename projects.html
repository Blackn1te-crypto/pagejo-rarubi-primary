School Projects

Hands-on learning that equips our learners with practical life skills and entrepreneurship.

Project 01

Mushroom Growing

Our mushroom project introduces learners to the fascinating world of fungi cultivation. Students learn about the entire lifecycle — from spawn preparation to harvesting and packaging.

  • Substrate preparation
  • Spawn inoculation
  • Climate control
  • Harvesting & packaging
Project 02

Growing of Pumpkins

Our pumpkin project is a favourite among learners. From preparing the seedbed to harvesting large, healthy pumpkins, students gain hands-on experience in vegetable farming.

  • Land preparation
  • Planting techniques
  • Watering methods
  • Pest control
Project 03

Road Runners Chicks

Our poultry project focuses on rearing road runner chicks. This project teaches learners animal husbandry, responsibility, and the economics of small-scale farming.

  • Brooder management
  • Feeding schedules
  • Health monitoring
  • Business records
